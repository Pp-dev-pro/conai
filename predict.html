02 How to read the result

1

Know the target

IS 10262 target mean strength = fck + 1.65 × s.

2

Allow for error

RMSE is about 5 MPa, so read a band, not a single number.

3

Decide

Clearly above target: cast cubes. Borderline: adjust and re-predict. Below: do not use.

4

Confirm

Use the app to narrow choices and the laboratory to confirm.
