CASE STUDY

Dam structures

Dams use huge volumes of concrete, so a small saving or a small mistake is multiplied by hundreds of thousands of cubic metres. This is an illustrative scenario, not the design of any particular dam.

01 Why dam concrete is different

1

Mass concrete

Thick blocks make heat, so cement is kept low and fly ash or slag replaces part of it.

2

Long design ages

Often 90 days or later, as pozzolanic materials gain strength slowly.

3

Large volumes

Many mixes are compared and many batches are produced daily.

4

Severe consequences

The structure must perform for decades.

05 Procedure, limits & conclusion

  • 1. Collect: list each candidate mix in kg/m³.
  • 2. Check: add the masses and compute w/cm.
  • 3. Predict: run each mix at 28, 90 and 180 days.
  • 4. Compare: subtract about 5 MPa error and compare with the target mean strength.
  • 5. Confirm: send only the best mixes for cube tests.

Limitations

  • The dataset is from 2007 and covers laboratory mixes; dam concrete uses larger aggregate and thick lifts.
  • Temperature rise and cracking in mass concrete are not predicted.
  • Errors average about 5 MPa; mixes outside the training ranges are unreliable.
  • The app is a screening tool, not a design or safety certificate.

Future scope

  • Train on recent and local Indian concrete data.
  • Compare deep learning models with Deep Forest.
  • Add cloud sync for prediction history.
  • Add a mix optimiser that suggests proportions for a target grade.

Conclusion: ConcreteAI brings strength prediction to the construction site. The Android app gives an estimate in seconds, the Catalyst website serves any browser, and laboratory testing remains the final proof.
