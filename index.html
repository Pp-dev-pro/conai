01 About the project

Concrete is the world's most used construction material, and its compressive strength tells an engineer whether a beam, slab or dam block is safe. Normally this number arrives only after cubes are cured for 7 or 28 days and crushed. Until then decisions are made on experience.


ConcreteAI gives an early, reasonably reliable estimate from seven ingredients (kg per m³) and the age in days. It is a screening tool: standard cube tests remain the proof of strength.

  • Build a simple Android app that predicts strength on site
  • Cut waiting time, trial mixes, material and testing cost
  • Offer the same service as a cloud website for laptops
  • Show through a dam case study how it supports real decisions

02 The real-life problem

PointTraditional testingConcreteAI (app and website)
TimeMixing, curing and compression tests take days to weeksEstimated in seconds from the mix inputs
CostRepeated trial batches and lab equipmentFewer trial mixes, less material and testing spend
AccuracyRebound hammer and ultrasonic checks are less preciseDeep Forest R² about 0.91 (app screen: 0.907)
DependenceNeeds a specialized lab and trained techniciansRuns on any Android phone or browser
AvailabilityOnly when the lab is open and cubes are readyAlways available (app works offline)

ConcreteAI gives an estimate. It supports decisions but does not replace cube tests and code requirements.

03 Who uses it

1

Site engineer

Enters batch quantities and age for an instant strength estimate before the next activity.

2

Ready-mix plant

Checks which proportion is likely to meet the target before running trial batches.

3

Contractor

Tries fly ash or slag replacement levels and sees the effect on strength at once.

4

Quality inspector

Cross-checks a contractor's mix before asking for further tests.

5

Student

Changes one input at a time to learn how water and age affect strength.

6

Dam teams

Screens mass-concrete mixes for strength at 28 to 365 days.
